COMPANY POLICIES AND PROCEDURES

1. REMOTE WORK POLICY
- Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Core hours: 9 AM - 3 PM EST must be observed
- Home office equipment allowance: $500 annually
- Regular check-ins with manager required

2. PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
- Annual learning budget: $2,000 per employee
- Conference attendance encouraged (up to 2 per year)
- Internal mentorship program available
- Tuition reimbursement for relevant courses (up to 50%)

3. TIME OFF POLICY
- Vacation days: 20 days annually (increases with tenure)
- Sick leave: 10 days annually
- Personal days: 5 days annually
- Parental leave: 12 weeks paid
- Mental health days: 3 additional days annually

4. PERFORMANCE REVIEW PROCESS
- Quarterly check-ins with direct manager
- Annual comprehensive review in December
- 360-degree feedback incorporated
- Career development planning included
- Promotion criteria clearly defined

5. COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES
- Slack for day-to-day communication
- Email for formal communications
- Video calls for complex discussions
- Response time: Within 4 hours during business hours
- After-hours communication should be minimal

6. CODE OF CONDUCT
- Respectful workplace environment
- Zero tolerance for harassment
- Confidentiality agreements must be honored
- Social media guidelines apply
- Conflict of interest disclosure required

7. BENEFITS OVERVIEW
- Health insurance: 90% company contribution
- Dental and vision: 80% company contribution
- 401(k) matching: Up to 6% of salary
- Life insurance: 2x annual salary
- Flexible spending accounts available

8. SECURITY POLICIES
- Two-factor authentication required
- VPN use mandatory for remote access
- Regular password updates (every 90 days)
- Data classification and handling procedures
- Incident reporting requirements
